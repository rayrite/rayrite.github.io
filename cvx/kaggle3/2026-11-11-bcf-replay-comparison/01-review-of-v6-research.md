## 1. Executive verdict

**Quality: a strong teaching artifact and a genuinely useful engine-idea source, but not scorer-parity evidence, and it carries one falsified task narrative, several misattributed citations, and config numbers that contradict the verified intel.** Adopt its throughput framing and four engine ideas; reject its specific thinking/output config, its "full tool list" remedy, and its fastapi_14482 story.

**Headline strengths**
- The core critique is right on the facts that matter: scoring default is **100** tool calls, not 50 (HR §7.1) `[V]`; the objective is **12 h / ~120 tasks ≈ 6 min/task** throughput, not single-task polish (synthesis fact 4, staff 743063/745805) `[V]`; **decode dominates wall-clock** (755291: 76% of agent wall; 02 §2) `[V]`; and the v5 call-count scorecard is **scripted [SIM]**, not a sampled result `[V]`.
- It inherits v5's honest evidence taxonomy and keeps the "one task, scripted decisions, exact trajectory ≈ 0%" caveat (v6-1 warnbox) `[V]`.
- httpx task facts are exact: 243-char statement, 7 gold files, and the five expected-red test lines **70/116/318/557/594** match the base `tests/test_parsers.py` byte-for-byte `[V]` (verified independently).
- Four engine ideas are **novel relative to the shipped brain** and attack real, measured gaps: expected-red predictor + asymmetry detector, caller-closure map + topological fix_order, scoped sed batching, and spec-transformed proxy tests (see §6). Our own comparison notes flag the first and third as the **high-importance divergences** (`notes-httpx-3672.md` §4 #3/#9).

**Headline weaknesses**
- **Honesty regression in v6-2/v6-3:** both drop the `[REPLAY]` class entirely (only `[V]/[SIM]/[EST]`), state tool outputs merely "reflect the real gold patch and hidden test [V]", yet present **"Container B verdict False/True"** scorecards and "shadow verdict" call cards with the same visual authority as v6-1's actually-replayed verdicts. No execution log backs them. Our discipline tags these `[MODELED]`/predicted and says "No grader run was performed" (notes header).
- **Falsified central claim in v6-3:** "both defects sit in `__post_init__` … one read sees both on adjacent lines" is **false** — the two loci are ~300 lines apart (`ModelField`/`__post_init__` at 64/95 vs `get_model_fields` at 419), and the deck's own `read_file(64,120)` cannot see the second. Gold patch is **5 hunks, not 4**. And fastapi_14482 is on the **27-task never-pass-locally list** (its test imports `inline_snapshot`, verified) — so its "True" verdict is not locally reproducible `[V]`.
- **Internal contradiction:** design claim #2 says "model routes, script measures … no rule router fitted to one task", but both fastapi decks show **task-fitted `rule_note`s** ("suspect shallow copy of response dicts"; "Pydantic v2 TypeAdapter config") doing the routing. Their `bcf.py` is not shipped, so this is `UNVERIFIED` but appears to contradict the doctrine.
- **Staleness:** silent on the E4B bonus track + thinking contest, the array-parser-hang doctrine, the SequentialAgent nudge-restart bug, compaction permanence/paraphrase doctrine, the masked-68 evaluation set, score noise, and queue ops. Its **thinking 256–512** recommendation contradicts the verified **2048** sweet spot and the thinking-off collapse (755066/746250/747914).
- **Misattributed citations:** "the deck's §9/§11 says this itself" (v5 contains no corpus-frequency concessions); "an unknown tool call is fatal (deck §2/§9.2)" (not in v5; the real source is competitor thread 747923); "rule router guesses 128/129" (our measured rule engine: kind 76%, family top-2 71.3%, localization hit@3 96.9%).
- The wall-clock model it cites as its "primary addition" (`../2026-11-11-bcf-wallclock-tuning/`) **does not exist anywhere in this workspace** (`find` returned nothing) — `UNVERIFIED`. The decode-dominance claim is nonetheless independently supported by our 02 §2.

**Net recommendation:** keep the shipped brain's reliability machinery and verified config; adopt the four engine ideas as pre-registered, masked-set A/B arms (§6); do not adopt the v6 config numbers or the fastapi-14482 narrative.
