## 11. Candidate B Profile: Mike Rogers (Republican Nominee)

### 11.1 Biographical Narrative & Michigan Roots
Michael J. Rogers was born on June 2, 1963, in Livonia, Wayne County, Michigan, the youngest of five sons born to Joyce and John Rogers. His father was a high school teacher, football coach, and small business owner, while his mother directed the Livingston County Chamber of Commerce. Rogers grew up in Howell, Livingston County, where he graduated from Howell High School in 1981, participating in student athletics and youth civic organizations.

Rogers attended Adrian College in Adrian, Michigan, where he earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Criminal Justice and Sociology in 1985. During college, he enrolled in the U.S. Army Reserve Officers' Training Corps (ROTC) through the University of Michigan detachment, receiving his commission as a second lieutenant upon graduation. From 1985 to 1989, Rogers served on active duty in the United States Army, rising to the rank of First Lieutenant and Company Commander within the 7th Infantry Division (Light) stationed at Fort Ord, California. During his active service, Rogers led rapid-deployment tactical units, receiving the Army Commendation Medal and the Army Achievement Medal.

Following his honorable discharge from the military in 1989, Rogers joined the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) as a Special Agent. Stationed in the Chicago Field Office from 1989 to 1994, Rogers worked in the Organized Crime and Public Corruption Unit, participating in undercover sting operations, financial fraud stings, and federal racketeering investigations targeting mob figures and corrupt municipal officials.

Rogers has two adult children, Erin and Jonathan, from his first marriage to Diane Rogers (which ended in divorce). In 2010, Rogers married Kristi Clemens Rogers, an accomplished national security and corporate executive who served as President and CEO of private security contractor Aegis Defense Services LLC and later as a high-ranking executive at ManTech International. In recent years, Rogers' primary residence has been situated in White Lake Township, Oakland County, Michigan, alongside a secondary residential property in Cape Coral, Florida.

### 11.2 Legislative & Congressional Record (1995–2015)
Rogers possesses 20 years of continuous elected legislative experience across Michigan state government and the federal legislature:
- **Michigan State Senate (1995–2001):** In 1994, Rogers was elected to the Michigan State Senate representing the 26th District (Livingston, Clinton, and Shiawassee counties), winning re-election in 1998. During his tenure, Rogers rose to the position of Senate Majority Floor Leader (1999–2000). He authored Michigan's Michigan Education Savings Program (MESP), enabling families to establish tax-advantaged college savings accounts, championed legislation stiffening criminal penalties for carjacking and repeat violent offenders, and sponsored regulatory reform measures supporting automotive suppliers.
- **U.S. House of Representatives (2001–2015):** Elected in 2000 to represent Michigan's 8th Congressional District (encompassing Lansing, East Lansing, Ingham County, Livingston County, and parts of Oakland and Clinton counties), Rogers served seven consecutive terms in the 107th through 113th Congresses.
  - **Committee Assignments:** Rogers served on the House Energy and Commerce Committee (Subcommittees on Health, Telecommunications, and Energy) and the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence (HPSCI).
  - **House Intelligence Committee Chairmanship (2011–2015):** In January 2011, Speaker John Boehner appointed Rogers Chairman of HPSCI, positioning him at the apex of congressional oversight of the nation's 16 intelligence agencies. Working closely with Ranking Member C.A. "Dutch" Ruppersberger (D-MD), Rogers established a reputation for bipartisan collaboration on national security. He steered multiple annual Intelligence Authorization Acts into law, reauthorized critical surveillance authorities, and led the committee's high-profile, two-year bipartisan investigation into the September 11, 2012 attacks on the U.S. diplomatic post in Benghazi, Libya. The resulting Rogers-Ruppersberger report concluded that CIA personnel acted heroically, that there was no "stand-down" order given to rescue teams, and that intelligence agencies did not deliberately mislead the public, debunking numerous partisan conspiracy theories.
  - **Key Legislative Enactments:** Rogers co-authored the Cyber Intelligence Sharing and Protection Act (CISPA), a landmark bill designed to facilitate cyber-threat information sharing between federal intelligence agencies and private telecommunications/tech infrastructure operators. He also authored the Respect for Fallen Heroes Act (prohibiting disruptive protests near military funerals) and was a leading congressional voice warning against Chinese corporate penetration of U.S. telecommunications networks, authoring a groundbreaking 2012 investigative report recommending that U.S. carriers exclude equipment from Huawei and ZTE.
- **Post-Congressional Private Sector Career (2015–2023):** After retiring from Congress in January 2015, Rogers established a successful private advisory career. He served as Chairman of the Board of Trustees for the Mitre Corporation, a federally funded national security research organization; advised cybersecurity venture capital firms (Forgepoint Capital, IronNet Cybersecurity); served on corporate boards; contributed as a national security analyst for CNN; and hosted the syndicated radio feature *Something to Think About* and CNN's original series *Declassified*.

### 11.3 Electoral & Political History

11. **2026 Michigan U.S. Senate Primary (August 4, 2026):** Consolidated party backing and ran unopposed for the Republican nomination.

### 11.4 The 18-Point Federal Policy Platform (RQ-05.1 – RQ-05.18)

Rogers' platform articulates mainstream conservative, national-security-oriented, and pro-business principles tailored to Michigan voters:

1. **Economy, Inflation, Trade & Tariffs (RQ-05.1):** Proposes making permanent the individual and small business tax reductions established under the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA). Attributes consumer inflation to excessive federal deficit spending and burdensome administrative agency regulations. Strongly opposes federal electric vehicle (EV) mandates, arguing they disadvantage Michigan automakers while empowering Chinese battery conglomerates. Supports aggressive, reciprocal tariffs on Chinese goods—especially automotive products transshipped via Mexico—to protect domestic manufacturing supply chains.
2. **Healthcare & Prescription Drug Pricing (RQ-05.2):** Advocates patient-centered, market-based healthcare reforms rather than government-administered medicine. Proposes expanding Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), permitting small businesses to band together through Association Health Plans (AHPs) across state lines, and mandating transparent, upfront pricing for hospital procedures and pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs). Fiercely opposes "Medicare for All," characterizing it as a $32 trillion socialist disruption that would strip over 5.5 million Michigan citizens of their employer-sponsored healthcare. Pledges to preserve Medicare solvency for current seniors without benefit cuts.
3. **Immigration, Border Security & Asylum (RQ-05.3):** Identifies the southern border crisis as a primary driver of the domestic fentanyl epidemic and transnational gang activity. Proposes completing the southern border wall, deploying advanced military-grade surveillance technologies, increasing U.S. Border Patrol staffing, and reinstating the Migrant Protection Protocols ("Remain in Mexico"). Advocates formally designating Mexican drug cartels as Foreign Terrorist Organizations (FTOs) to enable federal asset seizures and joint military-intelligence interdiction. Opposes blanket amnesty; supports reforming asylum laws to expedite immediate deportation of fraudulent claimants.
4. **Abortion & Reproductive Rights (RQ-05.4):** Maintained a consistent anti-abortion record during his 14 years in Congress, receiving a 100% legislative scorecard from National Right to Life (NRLC) and co-sponsoring federal fetal protection legislation. In the 2024 and 2026 cycles, Rogers adjusted his campaign stance, explicitly stating that Michigan voters definitively decided state abortion policy by passing Proposal 3 in 2022. Pledges that he will not vote for a federal abortion ban in the U.S. Senate, asserting that reproductive policy is a state-level issue under the 10th Amendment. Supports federal protections and tax credits for in vitro fertilization (IVF) procedures and over-the-counter contraception.
5. **Gun Policy & Gun Safety (RQ-05.5):** Staunch defender of Second Amendment rights; holds an "A" rating from the National Rifle Association (NRA) and endorsement from the NRA Political Victory Fund (NRA-PVF). Opposes federal bans on semi-automatic firearms (assault weapons) and standard-capacity magazines, arguing such measures disarm law-abiding citizens without deterring criminals. Opposes federal red flag mandates as violations of Fourth and Fifth Amendment due process. Focuses anti-violence policy on rigorous prosecution of violent felons under Project Safe Neighborhoods, enhanced mental health crisis resources, and federal grants to harden K-12 school physical security.
6. **Energy, Climate & the Great Lakes (RQ-05.6):** Champions an "all-of-the-above" domestic energy strategy to achieve American energy independence and lower industrial production costs. Advocates expanding domestic oil, natural gas, and next-generation advanced nuclear energy production. Vigorously defends the continued operation of Enbridge Line 5 through the Straits of Mackinac Tunnel Project, warning that shutting down the pipeline would spike home heating costs for 300,000 Upper Peninsula residents and disrupt regional refineries. Strongly supports the federal Great Lakes Restoration Initiative (GLRI), securing federal funding to combat invasive carp and maintain commercial shipping navigation channels.
7. **Taxes, Federal Budget & National Debt (RQ-05.7):** Calls the $35+ trillion national debt a clear and present threat to national security. Proposes establishing a bipartisan fiscal commission to address federal spending growth, enforcing statutory spending caps on non-defense discretionary appropriations, and clawing back unspent federal pandemic balances. Opposes corporate tax rate increases, arguing they reduce capital investment and drive high-tech manufacturing offshore.
8. **Social Security & Medicare Solvency (RQ-05.8):** Formally pledges not to cut Social Security or Medicare benefits for current retirees or workers within 10 years of retirement. Opposes Democratic proposals to lift the payroll tax cap on earnings above $400,000, arguing it represents a destructive tax hike on job-creating small business pass-through entities. Recommends structural bipartisan entitlement commissions modeled after the 1983 Greenspan Commission to protect long-term solvency through administrative efficiency and economic growth.
9. **Education, Student Debt & K-12 (RQ-05.9):** Advocates broad educational freedom and universal school choice, supporting federal legislation permitting Title I funds to follow students to public, charter, magnet, or private schools of their choice. Supports expanding Section 529 education savings plans to cover K-12 expenses and vocational apprenticeships. Opposes broad federal student loan forgiveness programs as unconstitutional bailouts funded by working-class Americans who did not attend college.
10. **Civil Rights, Equal Opportunity & Institutional Merit (RQ-05.10):** Supports traditional constitutional civil rights protections based on individual merit and equal opportunity. Opposes federal Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) mandates, racial quotas, and critical theory curricula in federal agencies, the military service academies, and federally funded universities. Supports requiring photo identification and proof of citizenship for federal voting. Advocates strong statutory protections for religious institutions and faith-based adoption agencies.
11. **Foreign Policy, Alliances & National Defense (RQ-05.11):** Premier campaign pillar rooted in "peace through strength." Supports robust, modern defense spending to maintain qualitative military supremacy over strategic adversaries. Reaffirms unwavering commitment to NATO while insisting European allies fulfill the 2% GDP defense spending threshold. Strongly supports military, financial, and intelligence aid to Israel in its defensive war against Hamas, Hezbollah, and Iranian proxies, opposing any conditioning of military assistance. Identifies the Chinese Communist Party as the foremost geopolitical adversary of the 21st century; advocates strategic economic decoupling in critical sectors (pharmaceuticals, rare earths, microchips), banning Chinese ownership of U.S. agricultural land near military bases, and enacting a national ban on TikTok due to national security and data privacy concerns.
12. **Judicial Appointments & Constitutional Interpretation (RQ-05.12):** Pledges to vote only to confirm federal judicial nominees committed to originalism and textualism in the mold of Supreme Court Justices Antonin Scalia, Clarence Thomas, and Neil Gorsuch. Strongly opposes progressive efforts to expand the size of the Supreme Court ("court packing"), impose legislative term limits on federal judges, or undermine judicial independence.
13. **Agriculture, Rural Policy & Farm Bill (RQ-05.13):** Heavily endorsed by the Michigan Farm Bureau AgriPAC. Advocates passing a robust, multi-year federal Farm Bill protecting federal crop insurance programs, risk management tools, and specialty crop research grants vital to Michigan fruit, vegetable, and dairy producers. Pledges to repeal burdensome EPA regulations, including excessive Waters of the United States (WOTUS) land restrictions. Supports expanding the H-2A agricultural guest-worker program to resolve acute seasonal labor shortages on Michigan farms.
14. **Housing & Urban Development (RQ-05.14):** Attributes housing unaffordability to local zoning restrictions, excessive environmental permitting delays, and high mortgage interest rates driven by federal spending. Opposes federal rent control mandates, which he argues suppress new construction. Proposes streamlining federal National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) permitting reviews to accelerate private residential development and providing regulatory relief for community banks to expand small-scale home construction lending.
15. **Labor, Workplace Freedom & Economy (RQ-05.15):** Strongly opposes the federal PRO Act, arguing it would infringe on worker privacy, eliminate right-to-work protections in 26 states, and disrupt independent gig-economy contracting. Emphasizes support for rank-and-file union members in manufacturing and skilled trades, focusing his pitch on protecting automotive jobs from EV mandates, cutting personal income taxes, and defending domestic manufacturing from foreign competition.
16. **Technology, Cybersecurity, AI & Section 230 (RQ-05.16):** Decades-long background in cybersecurity oversight (author of CISPA). Champions public-private cyber threat sharing to defend critical infrastructure (electrical grids, water systems, financial networks) from foreign state-sponsored hacking (Volt Typhoon, Russian cyber units). Advocates fundamental reform of Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act to hold Big Tech platforms accountable for censoring lawful political speech and failing to protect minors from harmful algorithmic content. Calls for strategic federal investments in advanced semiconductor manufacturing (CHIPS Act oversight) and AI development to ensure American supremacy over China.
17. **Law Enforcement, Crime & Criminal Justice (RQ-05.17):** High-profile "Back the Blue" advocate holding endorsements from statewide law enforcement organizations. Calls for substantial federal grants (COPS program) to hire and train local police officers, rejecting calls to defund the police or weaken qualified immunity. Advocates strict federal mandatory minimum sentences for fentanyl traffickers and firearm-related violent crimes. Supports the federal SAFE Banking Act to permit state-legal cannabis businesses access to regulated banking, while taking a cautious stance on full federal cannabis descheduling.
18. **Election Administration & Campaign Integrity (RQ-05.18):** Supports federal legislation requiring documentary proof of United States citizenship to register to vote in federal elections (the SAVE Act) and mandatory government photo ID at the ballot box. Defends state constitutional authority to oversee federal elections under Article I, Section 4. Defends First Amendment political speech rights of individual citizens and issue organizations, opposing federal campaign finance restrictions that regulate grassroots advocacy.
