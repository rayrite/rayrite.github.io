An examination of recent congressional district-level crosstabs from non-partisan tracking surveys (including Marist, Mitchell Research, Emerson College, and EPIC-MRA) illuminates the geographic realities of the race.

### 5.2 Deep-Dive on Pivotal Regional Battlegrounds

#### 1. The Oakland County Suburban Revolt (MI-11)
Oakland County represents the epicenter of Michigan’s anti-Trump suburban realignment. Historically the prosperous, tax-conscious backbone of the moderate Republican Party, Oakland has trended decisively Democratic since 2016.
- **The Trump Headwind:** In MI-11, Trump’s net favorability sits at **-15%** (39% favorable to 54% unfavorable). The demographic driving this trend is suburban college-educated women, who break **62% to 35% against Trump-aligned candidates**.
- **Impact on Rogers:** Despite Rogers residing in Oakland County (White Lake Township) and presenting a polished, corporate demeanor, Democrats’ persistent branding of Rogers as a "Trump rubber stamp" has severely compromised his suburban appeal. When polled on reproductive rights, 64% of Oakland County swing voters identify abortion access as a primary voting issue, heavily favoring El-Sayed (+16 on healthcare/reproductive freedom).
- **The Result:** El-Sayed leads Rogers **54% to 43%** in the district, forcing Rogers to spend heavily on broadcast television in the Detroit media market to mitigate suburban losses.

#### 2. The Macomb County Blue-Collar Counter-Offensive (MI-10)
Directly east of Oakland lies Macomb County, the historic home of blue-collar auto assembly workers and cultural conservatism.
- **The Trump Tailwind:** In MI-10, Trump remains exceptionally popular, holding a **+8% net favorability** (52% favorable to 44% unfavorable).
- **The Anti-EV Flashpoint:** Rogers’ message resonates powerfully in Macomb. By attacking the Biden-Harris administration's EPA emissions targets as "EV mandates" that will gut assembly line jobs at Warren Truck and Sterling Heights Assembly, Rogers has established an 11-point lead over El-Sayed (**54% to 43%**).
- **The Union Friction:** While the United Auto Workers (UAW) officially endorsed El-Sayed, polling indicates a significant split among rank-and-file union households in Macomb, with over 45% of union members favoring Rogers due to cultural conservatism, border security, and anxieties over EV plant conversions.

#### 3. The Shifting Sands of Western Michigan (MI-03 — Kent County)
Centered on Grand Rapids, Kent County was once the conservative financial and political fortress of Gerald Ford and the DeVos family. Over the past decade, however, rapid suburban growth, healthcare expansion, and an influx of educated young professionals have turned the county purple.
- **Trump Polarization:** In Kent County, Trump sits underwater at **-4%** (46% favorable to 50% unfavorable). While traditional business leaders remain loyal Republicans, suburban voters in Grand Rapids and surrounding townships express deep reservations regarding Trump’s rhetoric and election denialism.
- **Candidate Footprint:** El-Sayed leads Rogers narrowly in the 3rd District (**51% to 45%**). Rogers’ appearance with Trump at the Van Andel Arena consolidated the rural perimeter of the district but alienated moderate suburban independents who favored Peter Meijer in 2020.

#### 4. The Microcosm of Mid-Michigan (MI-07 — Lansing to Howell)
Michigan's 7th Congressional District encompasses Mike Rogers' former political territory, including his hometown of Howell in Livingston County, alongside Ingham County (Lansing and Michigan State University).
- **A Stark Internal Divide:**
  - *Ingham County:* Deep blue, heavily pro-El-Sayed (+28 margin), driven by state government employees and university students who view Trump with near-total antipathy.
  - *Livingston County:* Deep red, heavily pro-Trump and pro-Rogers (+24 margin), powered by exurban conservative professionals and small business owners.
- **The Balance:** Across the district as a whole, sentiment is virtually deadlocked, with El-Sayed holding a nominal **48% to 47%** edge.
