### Detailed Profiles of Core Federal Voter Portals

#### Vote.gov and USA.gov
- **Managing Entity:** Technology Transformation Services (TTS) within the U.S. General Services Administration (GSA), in statutory coordination with the U.S. Election Assistance Commission (EAC).
- **Functional Scope:** Vote.gov functions as the authoritative sovereign front-door for voter registration across the United States. It provides accessible, plain-language registration steps for all 50 states and territories, automatically detecting whether a user's state supports native Online Voter Registration (OVR), requires a downloadable National Mail Voter Registration Form, or operates under an exemption (e.g., North Dakota).
- **Language Access:** Provides localized user interfaces in English, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Vietnamese, Korean, Tagalog, Arabic, French, Haitian Creole, Portuguese, Russian, Hindi, and Navajo.
- **Data Integrity Standard:** All external links terminate exclusively on verified `.gov` state domains or official state election division subdomains.

#### U.S. Election Assistance Commission (EAC)
- **Statutory Mandate:** Created under the Help America Vote Act of 2002 (52 U.S.C. §§ 20901 et seq.) following the 2000 presidential election.
- **Core Voter Services:**
  1. **National Mail Voter Registration Form:** Maintains and updates the multi-lingual federal voter registration application accepted by most states under NVRA Section 9.
  2. **Voting System Testing and Certification:** Formulates Voluntary Voting System Guidelines (VVSG 2.0) and accredits independent Voting System Test Laboratories (VSTLs) to certify electronic voting machines, optical scanners, and ballot-marking devices.
  3. **Election Administration and Voting Survey (EAVS):** Biennial statutory survey publishing the most granular empirical datasets on voter turnout, registration roll maintenance, rejected ballots, poll worker shortages, and voting equipment usage by county.

#### Federal Voting Assistance Program (FVAP)
- **Statutory Mandate:** Administers the Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act (UOCAVA) of 1986 and the Military and Overseas Voter Empowerment (MOVE) Act of 2009.
- **Eligible Voters:** Uniformed service members on active duty, merchant marine personnel, Commissioned Corps of the Public Health Service and NOAA, and U.S. citizens residing outside the United States.
- **Core Digital Tools:**
  1. **Federal Post Card Application (FPCA):** The standardized federal form used simultaneously to register to vote and request an absentee ballot in all states and territories.
  2. **Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB):** An emergency backup ballot used by overseas voters who requested a regular ballot by the state deadline but have not received it within the statutory window.
  3. **Online Voting Assistance Guide:** Dynamic portal detailing specific state ballot request deadlines, return options (mail, email, fax, secure web portal), and notarization requirements.
