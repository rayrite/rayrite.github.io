## 5. State-level election portals (50 states + DC)

In the United States, primary constitutional authority over the times, places, and manner of holding federal and state elections resides with state legislatures (U.S. Const. art. I, § 4, cl. 1), executed through designated chief state election officers. This section delivers a uniform, exhaustive jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction operational audit across all 50 states and the District of Columbia.

Each jurisdictional profile contains direct canonical `.gov` links to the chief election officer, state election division, online voter registration (OVR), voter status lookup, polling place finder, sample ballot tools, official results archives, absentee/mail voting procedures, voter ID statutory citations, campaign finance databases, and accessibility resources.
