## 6. Major metropolitan / county / city election offices

In the decentralized American election system, the administrative mechanics of conducting an election—maintaining polling stations, procuring voting machines, training poll workers, mailing absentee ballots, and canvassing precinct votes—occur predominantly at the county or municipal level. For voters residing in large metropolitan areas, the county registrar or city board of elections is often the primary point of contact.

This section inventories the principal election offices for the top 50 U.S. Metropolitan Statistical Areas (MSAs) per the latest Census / OMB definitions, plus consolidated city-counties and major regional urban hubs (53 total metropolitan profiles). Each entry specifies the lead election authority, canonical portal links, localized lookups, campaign finance portals, and secondary county pointers across multi-county regional economies.
