## Key Architectural Takeaways for Ingestion Workflows

1. **Dual-Track Converters**: For ebook and document ingestion, `firecrawl/anydoc` provides high-throughput Rust bindings supporting direct EPUB-to-Markdown conversion, while `docling-project/docling` offers deep structural parsing (tables, math, reading order).
2. **Local Video Speech-to-Text**: For video workflows, `m-bain/whisperx` and `debpalash/voicestudio` eliminate cloud latency and privacy risks, providing word-level timestamps and multi-speaker diarization essential for LLM grounding.
3. **Direct Video RAG**: `video-db/videodb-python` and `ssrajadh/sentrysearch` pioneer index-once semantic video search, allowing developers to query moments in video using natural language without transcoding whole files.
