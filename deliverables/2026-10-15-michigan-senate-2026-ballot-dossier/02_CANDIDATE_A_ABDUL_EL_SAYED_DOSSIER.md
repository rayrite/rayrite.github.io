## 10. Candidate A Profile: Dr. Abdul El-Sayed (Democratic Nominee)

### 10.1 Biographical Narrative & Michigan Roots
Dr. Abdulrahman Mohamed El-Sayed was born on October 31, 1984, in Rochester Hills, Oakland County, Michigan, to Egyptian immigrant parents. His father, Mohamed El-Sayed, was an engineering professor at Wayne State University, and his mother, Jacqueline El-Sayed, served as a mechanical engineering educator and higher education administrator. Raised in the suburbs of northern Metro Detroit, El-Sayed attended Bloomfield Hills Andover High School, where he distinguished himself academically while captaining the varsity football, wrestling, and lacrosse teams, graduating in 2003.

El-Sayed attended the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor, majoring in biology and political science. At Michigan, he was elected to Phi Beta Kappa, served as a student activist, and was selected to deliver the student commencement address at his graduation in 2007. Upon graduating, El-Sayed was awarded a Rhodes Scholarship to study at Oriel College, University of Oxford, where he earned a Doctor of Philosophy (DPhil) in Public Health in 2011, focusing on social determinants of health and urban epidemiology. Concurrently, he completed his Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) degree at Columbia University’s Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons in New York City in 2014, where he also served as an assistant professor in the Mailman School of Public Health.

El-Sayed is married to Dr. Sarah Jukaku, a child and adolescent psychiatrist who grew up in Canton Township, Michigan. The couple married in 2006 and reside in Ann Arbor with their two young daughters, Emmalee and Serene. If elected, El-Sayed would make history as the first Muslim member of the United States Senate and one of the youngest senators serving in the 119th Congress.

### 10.2 Professional & Executive Public Service Record
Unlike traditional legislative candidates, El-Sayed's public career is rooted in executive municipal administration and public health crisis management:
- **Executive Director, Detroit Health Department (2015–2017):** In August 2015, at the age of 30, El-Sayed was appointed by Detroit Mayor Mike Duggan to rebuild the Detroit Health Department. The department had been privatized and dissolved under a non-profit contractor during Detroit's municipal bankruptcy. Reconstituting the agency as a fully municipal entity, El-Sayed expanded the municipal health budget from under $10 million to over $40 million, deploying maternal-infant health initiatives that reduced the city's infant mortality rate, establishing municipal lead screening protocols for all Detroit public schools and daycares, and creating a signature vision program that partnered with non-profits to test 30,000 students and distribute 10,000 pairs of free prescription glasses. He also took a high-profile stand against corporate polluters, publicly demanding stricter sulfur dioxide limits on the Marathon Oil refinery in Southwest Detroit.
- **Director, Wayne County Department of Health, Human & Veterans Services (2023–2026):** In February 2023, Wayne County Executive Warren Evans appointed El-Sayed to lead the county's consolidated health and human services division, overseeing an annual budget exceeding $150 million and 500+ employees across the state's most populous county. In this capacity, El-Sayed managed the post-pandemic transition, expanded county air-quality monitoring networks in environmental-justice corridors, expanded maternal health navigators to reduce Black maternal mortality, and modernized county veterans service delivery.
- **Academic & Media Appointments:** Between government roles, El-Sayed served as a Senior Fellow at the Roosevelt Institute, an adjunct professor at the University of Michigan's Gerald R. Ford School of Public Policy, and a political contributor on CNN. In 2019, he joined Crooked Media to create and host the weekly public health and science podcast *America Dissected*, which garnered millions of downloads and received a Webby Award.

### 10.3 Electoral & Political History
El-Sayed has mounted two high-profile statewide campaigns in Michigan:
1. **2018 Michigan Gubernatorial Democratic Primary (August 7, 2018):** El-Sayed launched an insurgent, progressive primary campaign for Governor against former State Senate Minority Leader Gretchen Whitmer and businessman Shri Thanedar. Endorsed by U.S. Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, El-Sayed campaigned on a detailed progressive platform ("Pure Michigan") including single-payer healthcare, renewable energy transitions, and campaign finance reform. El-Sayed finished in second place with **342,179 votes (30.2%)**, behind Whitmer (**588,436 votes, 52.0%**) and ahead of Thanedar (**200,645 votes, 17.7%**). Following the primary, El-Sayed actively campaigned for Whitmer in the general election.
2. **2026 Michigan U.S. Senate Democratic Primary (August 4, 2026):** Following Senator Gary Peters' retirement announcement, El-Sayed declared his candidacy for the open Class II seat. In a heated, three-way primary, El-Sayed was pitted against moderate, business-backed U.S. Representative Haley Stevens (MI-11) and progressive State Senator Mallory McMorrow (SD-08). Leveraging an army of 15,000 grassroots volunteers, the endorsement of the United Auto Workers (UAW), SEIU Michigan, and high turnout in Wayne, Washtenaw, and Ingham counties, El-Sayed won an upset victory with **744,952 votes (48.5%)**, defeating Stevens (**731,160 votes, 47.6%**) by 13,792 votes (+0.9%), while McMorrow secured **61,438 votes (4.0%)**.

### 10.4 The 18-Point Federal Policy Platform (RQ-05.1 – RQ-05.18)

El-Sayed's policy agenda combines democratic socialist and progressive populist principles tailored to Michigan's industrial and working-class economic realities:

1. **Economy, Inflation, Trade & Tariffs (RQ-05.1):** Proposes a comprehensive federal anti-price-gouging statute enforced by the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) to penalize corporate food and energy conglomerates during inflationary crises. Supports a $17/hour federal minimum wage indexed to inflation. Opposes trade pacts that lack enforceable labor and environmental standards; advocates aggressive targeted tariffs on subsidized Chinese industrial imports (including EVs and battery cells) to shield Michigan automotive manufacturing, while conditioning federal corporate subsidies on union domestic production guarantees.
2. **Healthcare & Prescription Drug Pricing (RQ-05.2):** Core signature platform. Author of *Medicare for All: A Citizen's Guide* (Oxford University Press). Advocates the enactment of the Medicare for All Act to establish a universal, single-payer national health insurance system eliminating out-of-pocket costs, copayments, deductibles, and private insurance gatekeeping. In the interim, supports expanding Medicare drug price negotiation to all prescription drugs under the Inflation Reduction Act, capping insulin at $35/month for all Americans, and creating a public manufacturer for generic pharmaceuticals.
3. **Immigration, Border Security & Asylum (RQ-05.3):** Supports comprehensive immigration reform providing an earned pathway to citizenship for the estimated 11 million undocumented immigrants and immediate permanent legal status for DACA recipients ("Dreamers"). Opposes physical border wall expansion; advocates smart border technology and increased judicial staffing to eliminate asylum processing backlogs. Calls for an end to family separations, private detention facilities, and the militarization of border enforcement agencies.
4. **Abortion & Reproductive Rights (RQ-05.4):** Strong supporter of Michigan's Proposal 3 (2022). Calls for the immediate federal codification of *Roe v. Wade* via the Women's Health Protection Act. Pledges to repeal the Hyde Amendment to ensure federal Medicaid covers abortion care. Supports federal statutory protection for in vitro fertilization (IVF), universal contraception access without co-pays, and federal shielding laws protecting patients and providers from cross-state abortion prosecution.
5. **Gun Policy & Gun Safety (RQ-05.5):** Approaches firearm violence through an epidemiological, public-health lens. Champions federal universal background checks on all firearm transfers (closing gun-show and online loopholes), a federal extreme risk protection order (red flag) statute, bans on civilian sales of assault weapons and high-capacity magazines, and repeal of the Protection of Lawful Commerce in Arms Act (PLCAA) to hold rogue firearm manufacturers legally accountable. Promotes significant federal grant investments in municipal Community Violence Intervention (CVI) programs.
6. **Energy, Climate & the Great Lakes (RQ-05.6):** Backs Green New Deal frameworks aiming for 100% clean electricity by 2035 and net-zero emissions economy-wide by 2050. Strongly supports the immediate decommissioning and shutdown of Enbridge Line 5 in the Straits of Mackinac to eliminate oil spill risks in the Great Lakes, proposing federal transition assistance for impacted Upper Peninsula propane markets. Advocates full federal funding for the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative (GLRI) at $500M annually and massive federal investments in PFAS water remediation and municipal lead service line replacements.
7. **Taxes, Federal Budget & National Debt (RQ-05.7):** Advocates rolling back the corporate tax cuts enacted under the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA), raising the corporate statutory rate from 21% to 28%. Proposes an annual wealth tax on net assets exceeding $50 million, closing the stepped-up basis loophole, and taxing capital gains as ordinary income for incomes exceeding $1 million. Supports permanent expansion of the federal Child Tax Credit (CTC) to $3,600 per child and expansion of the Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC).
8. **Social Security & Medicare Solvency (RQ-05.8):** Opposes any reduction in Social Security retirement age or benefit formulas. Co-sponsors the Social Security Expansion Act to lift the payroll tax cap on earnings above $400,000, which actuarially guarantees system solvency through the next 75 years while increasing monthly benefits by $200. Proposes expanding standard Medicare coverage to encompass dental, vision, hearing, and long-term home healthcare.
9. **Education, Student Debt & K-12 (RQ-05.9):** Advocates broad-based student debt cancellation and tuition-free public higher education, community colleges, and vocational-technical academies. Proposes tripling federal Title I funding for high-poverty K-12 school districts, establishing a $60,000 national minimum salary baseline for public school teachers, and universal, federally subsidized pre-K education for all three- and four-year-olds.
10. **Civil Rights, Voting Rights & LGBTQ+ Equality (RQ-05.10):** Calls for immediate passage of the John Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act and the Freedom to Vote Act to establish national standards for automatic voter registration, no-excuse mail voting, and an end to partisan gerrymandering. Strongly supports passage of the federal Equality Act to prohibit anti-LGBTQ+ discrimination in housing, employment, and public accommodations. Supports the George Floyd Justice in Policing Act to ban chokeholds, restrict qualified immunity, and establish a national police misconduct registry.
11. **Foreign Policy, Alliances & Global Security (RQ-05.11):** Promotes a restraint-oriented, multilateral foreign policy emphasizing diplomacy over military intervention. Reaffirms strong support for NATO and continued defense assistance to Ukraine against Russian territorial aggression. Takes a critical stance regarding Middle East policy: advocates conditioning U.S. offensive military assistance to Israel on compliance with international human rights law, an immediate and permanent ceasefire in Gaza, humanitarian surge operations, and a viable sovereign Palestinian state. Calls for diplomatic engagement with China alongside strong protection of international labor standards and Taiwan's democratic sovereignty.
12. **Judicial Appointments & Supreme Court Reform (RQ-05.12):** Pledges to confirm federal judges with records in civil rights, labor law, and public defense rather than corporate law backgrounds. Endorses structural Supreme Court reform, including 18-year term limits for justices, a binding and enforceable judicial ethics code overseen by an independent inspector, and open consideration of court expansion to counter conservative court-packing.
13. **Agriculture, Rural Policy & Farm Bill (RQ-05.13):** Proposes aggressive antitrust break-ups of multinational seed, fertilizer, and meatpacking monopolies (e.g., Tyson, JBS). Supports shifting federal Farm Bill subsidies away from mega-commodity conglomerates toward small, regenerative, family farms and specialty crop growers across Michigan. Advocates universal rural broadband deployment and federal subsidies to reopen and sustain rural community hospitals.
14. **Housing & Urban Development (RQ-05.14):** Treats housing as a basic human right. Proposes a federal investment of $100 billion into public and social housing development through a National Affordable Housing Trust Fund. Proposes federal restrictions and tax penalties on Wall Street private equity firms purchasing single-family residential properties, a federal tenant bill of rights, and first-generation homebuyer down payment grants up to $25,000.
15. **Labor, Unions & Worker Rights (RQ-05.15):** Uncompromising champion of organized labor; endorsed by the UAW. Strongly supports the immediate federal passage of the Richard L. Trumka Protecting the Right to Organize (PRO) Act to dismantle "right-to-work" laws nationally, ban employer captive-audience anti-union meetings, and penalize unfair labor practices. Supported Michigan's 2023 repeal of state right-to-work laws and restoration of prevailing wage statutes.
16. **Technology, AI, Antitrust & Privacy (RQ-05.16):** Advocates robust antitrust enforcement against Big Tech monopolies (Google, Amazon, Meta, Apple), including potential structural separation of platform operations from marketplace sales. Proposes a comprehensive European-style federal data privacy law granting consumers ownership over personal biometric and browsing data. Endorses federal regulatory frameworks for artificial intelligence requiring algorithmic transparency, civil-rights auditing, and protections against worker displacement.
17. **Cannabis & Criminal Justice Reform (RQ-05.17):** Advocates complete federal descheduling and legalization of cannabis, paired with federal restorative justice initiatives that automatically expunge non-violent federal cannabis convictions and direct federal tax revenue toward communities harmed by the War on Drugs. Opposes cash bail systems that incarcerate individuals based on poverty; supports repealing federal mandatory minimum sentencing statutes.
18. **Election Administration & Campaign Finance (RQ-05.18):** Core ideological pillar. Rejects corporate PAC contributions; advocates an amendment to the United States Constitution to overturn *Citizens United v. FEC*. Proposes a federal system of small-dollar public matching funds (6-to-1 match) for congressional campaigns to eliminate reliance on ultra-wealthy donors. Proposes mandating full real-time disclosure of all 501(c)(4) dark-money election spending.
