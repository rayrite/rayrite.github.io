### Appendix G: Incumbent Context — Sen. Gary C. Peters Retrospective

- **Electoral & Service History:** Elected to the U.S. Senate in 2014 (defeating Terri Lynn Land 54.6% to 41.3%); re-elected in 2020 in a competitive race against John James (49.9% to 48.2%, a margin of 92,335 votes). Prior to the Senate, Peters served in the U.S. House representing Oakland and Wayne counties (2009–2015), as Michigan Lottery Commissioner (2003–2008), and in the Michigan State Senate (1995–2002).

Championed federal cybersecurity legislation, Great Lakes restoration grants, and defense industrial base authorizations for Michigan military installations (Selfridge Air National Guard Base).
- **Party Leadership:** Chaired the Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee (DSCC) for two consecutive election cycles (2022 and 2024), successfully defying historical midterm headwinds to defend the Democratic Senate majority in 2022.
- **Retirement Declaration:** Formally announced that he would not seek a third six-year Senate term in 2026, creating Michigan's open Class II Senate contest. Peters endorsed Haley Stevens in the Democratic primary, but formally endorsed nominee Abdul El-Sayed following the August canvass.
