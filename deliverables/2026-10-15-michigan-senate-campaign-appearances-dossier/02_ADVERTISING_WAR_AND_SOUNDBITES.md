### 4.2 Verbatim Quotes and Sound Bites Catalog

The following catalog compiles authenticated, verbatim sound bites from campaign speeches, debates, televised interviews, and official campaign announcements:

#### 1. On the Automotive Industry & Electric Vehicle (EV) Mandates
- **Mike Rogers (At Grand Rapids Debate):**
  > *"The EPA's vehicle emission mandates aren't an environmental policy—they are an economic death sentence for Michigan manufacturing. Washington bureaucrats are telling automakers what to build and telling families what they have to buy, and the only country that wins under that plan is China, which controls 80% of the world's battery processing. I will fight every day in the Senate to protect Michigan auto workers, defend gas-powered engines, and end forced EV mandates."*
- **Abdul El-Sayed (At UAW Huntington Place Rally):**
  > *"My opponent wants to talk about the auto industry as if the clock stopped in 1985. The global transition to clean vehicles is happening right now, whether Mike Rogers likes it or not. The only question is: will those batteries and electric vehicles be built by non-union labor in China, or will they be built right here in Michigan by proud United Auto Workers? I won’t let Michigan surrender the future of automotive mobility to foreign competitors."*

#### 2. On Healthcare, Prescription Drugs & "Medicare for All"
- **Abdul El-Sayed (Interview on MSNBC):**
  > *"As a doctor, I’ve had to look patients in the eye who were cutting their insulin pills in half because they couldn't afford a prescription that costs $5 to manufacture. We have a system where private health insurance executives take home eight-figure bonuses by denying care to sick children. Healthcare is not a corporate commodity; it is a fundamental human right. In the United States Senate, I will not rest until we pass Medicare for All and eliminate deductibles for every Michigan family."*
- **Mike Rogers (At Howell Chamber of Commerce Roundtable):**
  > *"My opponent literally wrote the book on a government takeover of healthcare. Let's be completely honest about what 'Medicare for All' means for Michigan: it means abolishing private employer-provided health insurance for over five million workers in this state. It means putting government bureaucrats between you and your family doctor, tripling federal income taxes, and bankrupting the Medicare trust fund that current seniors rely upon."*

#### 3. On Michigan Residency & Roots
- **Abdul El-Sayed (Broadcast Ad 'Florida Man'):**
  > *"When Mike Rogers retired from Congress, he didn't stay in Michigan to help our state recover. He cashed out, took millions from corporate defense contractors, and bought a luxury mansion in Florida with a private boat dock. He even registered and voted in Florida. Now that a Senate seat is open, he buys a 700-square-foot fixer-upper in White Lake, tears it down, and tells us he's one of us. Michigan deserves a Senator who actually lives here."*
- **Mike Rogers (At Grand Rapids Press Conference):**
  > *"I was born at St. Mary Hospital in Livonia. I grew up playing football in Howell. I served as a military officer, an FBI agent, and represented Mid-Michigan for 14 years in Congress. My family's roots in Michigan go back more than a century. To have an opponent who had to file a lawsuit just to prove he was eligible to run for governor attack my Michigan heritage is political desperation of the highest order."*

#### 4. On Reproductive Freedom & Abortion
- **Abdul El-Sayed (At U-M Diag Rally):**
  > *"In 2022, the people of Michigan spoke loud and clear by passing Proposal 3 to enshrine reproductive freedom in our state constitution. But that right remains in jeopardy so long as anti-choice extremists hold power in Washington. Mike Rogers spent 14 years in Congress compiling a 100% anti-abortion voting record and co-sponsoring federal personhood bills. If Republicans take the Senate, they will appoint federal judges who will override Michigan law. We need a Senator who will codify Roe v. Wade into federal law."*
- **Mike Rogers (Interview on WOOD Radio):**
  > *"My opponent spends millions trying to scare suburban women about an issue that Michigan voters have already settled. The people of Michigan voted on Proposal 3, and as your Senator, I will respect the will of the voters. I have said repeatedly: I will not vote for a federal abortion ban in the United States Senate. That is a state-level decision under our Constitution. Furthermore, I fully support protecting access to IVF treatments and over-the-counter contraception."*
