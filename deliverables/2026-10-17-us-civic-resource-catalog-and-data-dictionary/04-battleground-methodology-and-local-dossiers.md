## 1. Battleground State Classification Methodology (2026 Midterm Cycle)

### Dynamic Status Determination Standard
In adherence to Section 8 of the research handoff prompt, battleground status is treated as **election-specific, office-specific, and temporally bound** rather than a static historical label. For the 2026 midterm election cycle, statewide competitiveness was evaluated by synthesizing ratings from three premier independent election analytics organizations alongside recent certified election returns:
1. **The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter:** 2026 Senate and Gubernatorial Trackers (published Q3 2026).
2. **Sabato's Crystal Ball (UVA Center for Politics):** 2026 Midterm Outlook and Senate/Gubernatorial Ratings.
3. **Inside Elections with Nathan L. Gonzales:** 2026 Battleground Ratings and House Vulnerability Matrix.
4. **Recent Electoral Precedent:** 2020–2024 Presidential, Senate, and Gubernatorial certified victory margins within 3.0 percentage points.

## 3. Sub-State Local Civic Portals and Data Repositories

The following directory documents verified, primary sub-state portals across the 14 core battleground metropolitan centers:

### 1. Arizona Local Dossiers
* **Maricopa County (`AZ-MAR`):**
  * *Election & Voter Portal:* Maricopa County Elections Department (`elections.maricopa.gov`) & BeBallotReady Portal (`beballotready.vote`). Handles 2.5 million registered voters, vote center mapping, and ballot tracking.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Maricopa County Superior Court Clerk (`clerkofcourt.maricopa.gov`). Public docket index, criminal filings, civil dockets.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Maricopa County Attorney’s Office (`maricopacountyattorney.org`). Policy memos, felony charging dashboards, victim services.
  * *Local Governance:* Phoenix City Clerk & City Council (`phoenix.gov/cityclerk`). Agendas, ordinances, council meeting minutes, and municipal lobbyist filings.
* **Pima County (`AZ-PIM`):**
  * *Election & Voter Portal:* Pima County Recorder (`recorder.pima.gov`) and Pima County Elections (`pima.gov/elections`). Early ballot requests and precinct maps.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Pima County Superior Court (`sc.pima.gov`). Civil, criminal, and probate dockets.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Pima County Attorney's Office (`pcao.pima.gov`). Special operations, criminal diversion guidelines.

### 2. Georgia Local Dossiers
* **Fulton County (`GA-FUL`):**
  * *Election & Voter Portal:* Fulton County Registration & Elections (`fultoncountyga.gov/services/elections`). Early voting hubs, drop boxes, local sample ballots.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Fulton County Clerk of Superior Court (`fultonclerk.org`). E-filing portal, criminal indictments, civil case search.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Fulton County District Attorney (`fultoncountyga.gov/departments/district-attorney`). High-profile public integrity trials, grand jury indictments.
  * *Local Governance:* Atlanta City Council & City Clerk (`citycouncil.atlantaga.gov`). Council agendas, legislative tracking, municipal code.
* **Chatham County (`GA-CHA`):**
  * *Election & Voter Portal:* Chatham County Board of Elections (`elections.chathamcountyga.gov`). Savannah voter registration, precinct returns.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Chatham County Clerk of Superior Court (`chathamcourts.org`). Felony dockets, land records.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Eastern Judicial Circuit District Attorney (`chathamda.com`). Charging releases, victim witness assistance.

### 3. Michigan Local Dossiers
* **Wayne County & Detroit (`MI-WAY`):**
  * *Municipal Election Authority:* Detroit Department of Elections (`detroitmi.gov/departments/elections`). Led by City Clerk Janice Winfrey; administers 500+ precincts, absentee counting boards, and vote centers.
  * *County Election Authority:* Wayne County Clerk (`waynecounty.com/elected/clerk`). Canvassing board, county campaign finance records.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Third Judicial Circuit Court of Michigan (`3rdcc.org`). Largest trial court in Michigan; civil, criminal, and family dockets.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Wayne County Prosecutor’s Office (`waynecounty.com/elected/prosecutor`). Felony intake, Conviction Integrity Unit (CIU).
* **Kent County & Grand Rapids (`MI-KEN`):**
  * *Municipal Election Authority:* Grand Rapids City Clerk (`grandrapidsmi.gov/Government/Departments/City-Clerk-Office`). Municipal voting sites.
  * *County Authority & Court:* Kent County Clerk & 17th Circuit Court (`accesskent.com`). Unified county portal, docket search, campaign finance.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Kent County Prosecutor (`accesskent.com/Prosecutor`). Annual crime reports, charging policies.

### 4. Nevada Local Dossiers
* **Clark County (`NV-CLA`):**
  * *Election & Voter Portal:* Clark County Election Department (`clarkcountynv.gov/election`). Comprehensive mail ballot tracking, registered voter statistics, polling places for Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Eighth Judicial District Court (`clarkcountycourts.us`). Odyssey portal for civil, criminal, and family dockets.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Clark County District Attorney (`clarkcountynv.gov/district-attorney`). Special prosecutions, grand jury indictments.
* **Washoe County (`NV-WAS`):**
  * *Election Authority:* Washoe County Registrar of Voters (`washoecounty.gov/voters`). Reno/Sparks election administration.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Second Judicial District Court (`washoecourts.com`). Searchable public dockets, civil case histories.

### 5. North Carolina Local Dossiers
* **Mecklenburg County (`NC-MEC`):**
  * *Election Authority:* Mecklenburg County Board of Elections (`meckboe.org`). Charlotte vote centers, candidate filing lists.
  * *Court & Dockets:* 26th Judicial District / eCourts Odyssey Portal (`nccourts.gov/counties/mecklenburg-county`).
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Mecklenburg County District Attorney (`charmeckda.com`). Habitual felon prosecution, homicide team filings.
* **Wake County (`NC-WAK`):**
  * *Election Authority:* Wake County Board of Elections (`wake.gov/elections`). Raleigh voter guidance, early voting schedule.
  * *Court & Dockets:* 10th Judicial District / eCourts (`nccourts.gov/counties/wake-county`).
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Wake County District Attorney (`nccourts.gov/wake-district-attorney`). Public integrity investigations.

### 6. Pennsylvania Local Dossiers
* **Philadelphia County (`PA-PHI`):**
  * *Election Authority:* Philadelphia City Commissioners (`philadelphiavotes.com`). Three-member bipartisan board; administers voting machines, mail-in drop-off sites, and official canvass.
  * *Court & Dockets:* First Judicial District of Pennsylvania (`courts.phila.gov`). Common Pleas and Municipal Court dockets; criminal records.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Philadelphia District Attorney’s Office (`phila.gov/district-attorney`). Transparency dashboard, Conviction Integrity Unit.
* **Allegheny County (`PA-ALL`):**
  * *Election Authority:* Allegheny County Elections Division (`alleghenycounty.us/elections`). Pittsburgh precincts, ballot return sites.
  * *Court & Dockets:* Fifth Judicial District of Pennsylvania (`alleghenycourts.us`). Dockets, civil filing system, arbitration records.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Allegheny County District Attorney (`alleghenycountyda.us`). Criminal case releases, diversion initiatives.

### 7. Wisconsin Local Dossiers
* **Milwaukee County & City (`WI-MIL`):**
  * *Municipal Authority:* City of Milwaukee Election Commission (`city.milwaukee.gov/election`). Voting wards, Central Count absentee tabulation.
  * *County Authority:* Milwaukee County Election Commission (`county.milwaukee.gov/County-Clerk/Election-Commission`). Countywide certification.
  * *Court & Dockets:* First Judicial District / Wisconsin CCAP (`wcca.wicourts.gov`). Criminal, civil, and small claims dockets.
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Milwaukee County District Attorney (`county.milwaukee.gov/DA`). Case processing metrics, community prosecution.
* **Dane County & Madison (`WI-DAN`):**
  * *Municipal Authority:* City of Madison City Clerk’s Office (`cityofmadison.com/clerk`). Democracy in the Park ballot drop-off sites, voter registration.
  * *County Authority & Court:* Dane County Clerk (`countyofdane.com/clerk`) and 5th Judicial District (CCAP).
  * *Prosecutorial Authority:* Dane County District Attorney (`countyofdane.com/da`). Restorative justice programs, victim assistance.
