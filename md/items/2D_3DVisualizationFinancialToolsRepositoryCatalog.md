# 2D/3D Visualization & Financial Tools Repository Catalog

> **Scope.** This catalog is curated exclusively from the supplied `repo_showcase_merged.json` dataset. It covers 2D and 3D visualization, dashboards and visual analytics, CAD and geospatial visualization, financial data and research tooling, backtesting, portfolio/accounting tools, financial modeling, and fintech infrastructure. Repository metadata and star counts are source snapshots rather than live assessments. [1]

## Financial-Use Boundary

> This is a catalog of software projects, not investment research, a trading recommendation, or personalized financial advice. Autonomous live-trading agents and unrelated consumer projects were excluded; the quant category emphasizes research and backtesting infrastructure. [1]
