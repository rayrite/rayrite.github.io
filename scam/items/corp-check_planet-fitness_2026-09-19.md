## The Record by Theme

### Regulator & enforcement
- **2016-01-14 · NY AG settlements — 96 health club operators** (AG Schneiderman; Health Club Law investigation): "failures to make required disclosures" and "unenforceable contract releases and liability waivers"; terms = contract modifications + penalties. **Penalty scale $250 per single-location operator; named chain leaders Curves ($2,650) and LA Fitness ($6,750); Planet Fitness not named in CBS's account** (one Patch listing includes it — discrepancy noted). Tiered: **minor, ministerial, stale.** [Documented — CBS, fetched 2026-09-19]

### Litigation & class actions
- **LIVE · *Matsunaga v. Planet Fitness, Inc., et al.*, 1:26-cv-00576 (D.N.H.)** — securities fraud class action: filed 2026-07-14; Judge Paul J. Barbadoro; NOS 850, jury demanded; defendants PF Inc., CEO Keating, CFO Stasz. Alleges false/misleading statements re: the 2024 strength-training marketing shift, net member growth, and FY2026 guidance, culminating in the 2026-05-07 guidance cut and ~31% one-day stock drop to $44.01. Class period 2025-11-06→2026-05-06; LP deadline 2026-09-14 (passed); no appointment recorded as of 2026-09-19. [Documented — KTMC + six firms' case pages; PacerMonitor/Justia listings; docket not directly opened]
- ***Truglio v. Planet Fitness*, 3:15-cv-07959 (D.N.J.) — DISMISSED.** Filed 2015-09 (NJ state; removed 2015-11); alleged unclear cancellation-cost disclosure with contradictory terms ("by the 10th of the month" vs. "thirty days' notice"); most claims dismissed 2017-08-21 (no ascertainable loss); final TCCWNA claim dismissed **2018-12-21** — 360 F. Supp. 3d 274 (D.N.J.) — docket's last activity 2019-03-29. **No settlement, no certification — PF prevailed.** [Documented — court opinion via Justia/openJurist]
- **COVID-19 fee class actions — compelled to arbitration / dismissed (2021), no settlement, no payment.** *Holloway v. Planet Fitness* (N.D. - **NJ wrongful-death suit** (individual; vehicle crashed into facility; safety-barrier allegations). [Reported] Individual suit — not a pattern signal.

### Dark-pattern & deception record (DP-ID mapped)
- **DP-SUB-03 channel asymmetry [Documented-policy]** — official mechanics: join online in minutes; cancel by **mail to your home club (or in-person form), submitted by the 10th of the month, up to 7 business days to process** (planetfitness.com official snippets, ×3 consistent queries; direct fetch 403'd). Commitment terms carry a ~$58 buyout; the annual fee is avoided only by cancelling by ~the 25th of the prior month (guides). *Gray-zone tension, stated honestly:* the mail requirement is disclosed — the pattern call rests on the join-fast/exit-slow asymmetry, the same conduct family the FTC's (vacated 2025-07) click-to-cancel rule targeted and its 2025-08 LA Fitness suit alleges. **No enforcement names PF on this conduct.**
- **DP-SUB-06 zombie billing [Reported]** — BBB complaint arc includes continued charges after cancellation requests; T5 complaint level only.
- **DP-SUB-05 contradictory terms — mooted:** the *Truglio* allegation was dismissed (2018); no live matter carries it. (The official FAQ's "by the 10th" processing advice echoes the attacked term — now framed as guidance.)
- **Gray zones (no DP mapping, per register rules):** the **annual fee (~$39–54)** is a disclosed contract term — surprise ≠ concealment; "advertised $10/month vs. ~$39 first-year reality" is pricing friction, not documented deception; honest-but-slow processing is not DP-SUB-10 doom loops (no engineered runaround documented).
- **Unresolved:** Reuters (2025-08-20) reported PF "gave members the ability to cancel online this year"; PF's own language is mail-centric. Primary verification blocked (403). Open, decision-relevant only to prospective members.

## Balanced Synthesis

**What the total picture supports:** Planet Fitness is a strong consumer value franchise whose adverse paper trail is dominated by (1) one **pending investor-facing** securities suit — allegations, unresolved, naming the CEO and CFO — and (2) **membership-mechanics friction**: a mail-based, by-the-10th, up-to-7-business-days cancellation path (DP-SUB-03 family, disclosed but asymmetric), annual-fee timing rules, zombie-billing complaint reports (T5), and a BBB F earned by not answering complaints rather than by adjudicated conduct. Stale minor items (2015 tanning settlement; 2016 $250-scale sweep) round out the regulator record. **What it does not support:** "Planet Fitness is a deceptive gym" — nothing adjudicated or formally resolved against PF on consumer deception exists in the record; every consumer class action that reached an ending ended in PF's favor or arbitration without payment. Nor does it support "clean" — the live securities suit, the real friction arc, and the unanswered-complaint posture are documented. The pair 🟠/Mixed is the honest verdict; on the consumer-deception axis alone the record reads 🟡 (friction signals, disclosed policies, dismissed suits).

## Consumer Takeaways

1. **Cancellation is the documented friction point:** mail to your home club (or in-person form), **by the 10th of the month, up to 7 business days processing** — send early, use certified mail if distance matters, and keep written confirmation. If you joined online, ask about your account's cancellation path before you need it.
2. **Know your annual-fee date** — cancel by ~the 25th of the prior month or expect the ~$49 charge. No-commitment tiers avoid the ~$58 buyout issue.
3. **No live consumer class action found**; both consumer suits that reached an outcome ended without payment by PF. The active litigation (*Matsunaga*) is investor-facing — relevant only if you hold PLNT stock (class period 2025-11-06 → 2026-05-06; LP deadline passed 2026-09-14).
4. The gym experience itself carries a strong documented reputation — value, cleanliness, staff, franchisee satisfaction at ~20.7M-member scale.
5. Not legal or investment advice; pending allegations are allegations until resolved.
